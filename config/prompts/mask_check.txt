Look only at what the tint and outline actually cover in the right halves, compare it with the claim, and answer with a single JSON object and nothing else:
{"inside": "...", "outside": "...", "covers": "...", "verdict": "...", "reason": "..."}

- inside: what is visibly inside the tinted/outlined area (colours, shapes, parts).

- reason: one short sentence for the verdict. No other output.
